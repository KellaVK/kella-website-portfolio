Kella Kruebbe is a young magician out of New Orleans, Louisiana that has been performing magic since before his first memories solidified. Over the years he has had the privilege of studying under some of the greatest minds in magic, including David Blaine, Michael Carbonaro, Mac King, Mike Caveney, Michael Dardant, Neil Baum, and more.

Kella performs mind-blowing magic for private rendezvous' all over the capital of voodoo, extracting deafening woohoo’s from crowds of all ages.

In the event YOUR EVENT needs a bit of flair that people WILL talk about, do not risk the lifelong regret of missing his exhibition of the mystical arts.
